# Dissipative-anomaly draft (not for submission)

This draft will not be submitted. The simulations behind it ran in a weakly
turbulent state, where the Hermite sector evolves almost entirely by linear
phase mixing. The ν-independent ε_ν it reports is therefore the linear result
already known from Zocco & Schekochihin (2011) and Kanekar et al. (2015,
J. Plasma Phys. 81, 305810104), not a dissipative anomaly in nonlinear
turbulence, and the abstract's claim of a nonlinear benchmark does not hold.

The draft is kept as a record of Study 02. See
`studies/02-collisionality-scan/docs/status_summary.md` for how the study was
closed.
