# Truncated Hermite hierarchy: self-checking scripts

These scripts check the results on GANDALF's truncated Hermite hierarchy against the installed `krmhd` package (read-only): its exact decomposition into M+1 passive slices, the phase velocities, the conserved forms, the dictionary between slice correlations and the Hermite spectrum (with Kanekar et al. 2015's forced spectrum at finite Lambda), the Hermite flux and the sine-kernel form of its pair weight, hypercollisional reflection at the closure, and what the slices imply for time stepping. Each script prints PASS or FAIL for every check and exits with status 1 if any check fails. CMM is Chandran, Mallet & Meyrand (2026, arXiv:2607.27981v1). Independent checks of the same results are in `../review/truncated/`.

| script | what it checks | paper section | run time |
|---|---|---|---|
| `t1_hierarchy.py` | GANDALF's hierarchy is exactly M+1 passive slices: streaming matrix, symmetrising weight, slice equation on `krmhd_rhs`, no leakage between slices through `gandalf_step` except by hypercollisions | Sections 2, 7 and 8, Appendix C | 20 s |
| `t2_eigenvalues.py` | the phase velocities u_j: Gauss-Hermite limit, determinant identity and sum rules, spectral-shift law, zero eigenvalue at even M, u_max, the size of its Lambda shift (about 1e-49 at M = 64) and its asymptotics, conventions | Sections 7 and 8, Appendix C | 12 s |
| `t3_invariants.py` | the conserved quadratic forms Q_phi, their identification with CMM's W, Gamma and H, the helicity at odd and even M, the weight of the node u = 0 and the size of the even-M error | Sections 7 and 8, Appendix C | 4 s |
| `t4_dictionary.py` | the dictionary between slice correlations and W(m) in the continuum, the closed form at Lambda = inf, the Lambda-independent asymptote and the power-law correspondence; part E (the truncated, slice-diagonal form) is not in the current paper | Sections 5 and 8 | 10 s |
| `t4b_kanekar_exact.py` | Kanekar et al. (2015) eq. (4.10) at finite Lambda is exactly the spectrum of delta-correlated slices with e proportional to rho; the m = 1 departures (4.7%, 5.2%) from (1 - 1/Lambda) times the Lambda = inf closed form; the injection rate carried through every m by the antisymmetric part of the correlation (mpmath, 30 digits) | Sections 5 and 8 | 182 s |
| `t5_flux_cd.py` | the Hermite flux as Christoffel-Darboux-filtered dephasing of slice pairs, that krmhd's `hermite_flux` is this flux with the full grad_par (krmhd 0.7.0), and its sine-kernel limit at Lambda = inf | Sections 5 and 8 | 3 s |
| `t5b_pair_weight.py` | the sine-kernel form of the continuum Christoffel-Darboux kernel and of the pair weight; at finite Lambda the pair-weight deviation stays of order 1/pi as m grows (continuum recurrence and GANDALF's ladder eigenvectors at M = 1500) | Section 5 | 2 s |
| `t6_reflection.py` | the hypercollisional reflection law R = exp(-X), and the forced steady state, where W_zero/W_open tends to coth X at low m | not in the current paper | 41 s |
| `t7a_cfl_slices.py` | the explicit advection of slice j by Phi + s u_j Psi, GANDALF's CFL estimate, which includes u_j (krmhd 0.7.0), the instability of the u_max slices under a limit without u_j, and the stability of the explicit stages (Lawson-RK4 stable for y <= 2 sqrt2 on the imaginary axis) | Sections 7 and 8 (the IMEX-RK222 part concerns the time-stepping appendix of an earlier draft) | 25 s |
| `t7b_imex_order.py` | the second-order coupling of g to z+- in GANDALF's IMEX-RK222 step (krmhd 0.7.0; first order with the stage time of krmhd 0.6.0) and the second-order drift of the invariants | not in the current paper | 15 s |

`tcore.py` holds the helpers shared by the scripts. Run times are wall times of one run on an Apple M1 Pro (Python 3.14, krmhd 0.7.0, CPU, float64); the whole folder took 314 s. `t1_hierarchy.py`, `t7a_cfl_slices.py` and `t7b_imex_order.py` integrate in time with `gandalf_step` and list each integration (scheme, grid, M, steps, wall time) in their output.

Run the folder from the repository root with `bash paper/phase-velocity-slices/derivations/truncated/run_all.sh`, which runs each script `t<n>*_*.py` with `uv run python`, writes its output to `out/<script>.out`, records the start time and wall time of each script in `out/run_all.out` and exits non-zero if any script fails. A single script runs as `uv run python paper/phase-velocity-slices/derivations/truncated/<script>.py`.
