\section{Notation}\label{app:notation}

Table~\ref{tab:conventions} compares our notation with that of CMM \citep{Chandran2026}, S09 \citep{Schekochihin2009} and the GANDALF code \citep{Kanekar2025}. Three symbols mean different things in the three papers. Our $\kappa$ is the wavenumber of the compressive fluctuations along the perturbed field, which S09 (\S6.3) call $k_{\parallel0}$; CMM use $\kappa$ for the square root that we call $\Delta$. Our $\chi$ is the nonlinearity parameter, whereas in S09 and CMM $\chi$ is the gyrokinetic potential. Our $W(m)$ is the Hermite spectrum of one branch, whereas in S09 and CMM $W$ is the free energy of the whole plasma, which S09 call the generalized energy. S09 also use $\zeta$ twice, for the Elsasser potentials $\zeta^\pm$ and for the phase velocity $\zeta_i$ of a linear mode.
